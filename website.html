The Black Swan Origin.

How Team G-04 decided to solve SOC alert fatigue.

"We didn't just want to log attacks. We wanted to build the intuition that standard SIEMs lack."

The Problem: In modern Security Operations Centers (SOCs), analysts are drowning in data. Legacy SIEMs rely entirely on rigid, signature-based rules, generating thousands of false positives daily. This creates "alert fatigue," where critical threats slip through the cracks simply because humans cannot process the sheer volume of noise.

The Black Swan Event: In cybersecurity, a "Black Swan" is an unpredictable, unprecedented event with catastrophic consequences—like an undetected zero-day exploiting an unsecured IoT ecosystem. To stop a Black Swan, you cannot rely on old signatures. You need a system that can adapt, reason, and correlate behavior autonomously.

The Genesis: Under the supervision of Prof. Dr. Ibrahim Attiya and Eng. Nesma at Mansoura University, Project G-04 was formed. We designed ARES (originally codenamed Black Swan) as a hybrid platform. By fusing deterministic network packet analysis with a neuro-symbolic AI brain, ARES acts as an autonomous digital analyst—intercepting anomalies, mapping them to MITRE ATT&CK, and securing the future.

Engineered for Absolute Defense.

Combining deterministic packet analysis with artificial intuition.

Neuro-Symbolic Coordinator.

The central nervous system of ARES. It processes network telemetry natively, invokes highly-optimized detection engines, correlates potential multi-stage attacks, and automatically generates incident narratives mapped directly to MITRE ATT&CK frameworks.

ODIN AI Model.

Advanced telemetry analysis leveraging custom machine learning deployed in secure IoT ecosystems to detect hardware and flow anomalies instantaneously.

Dynamic Sandbox.

An isolated honeypot environment actively designed to attract, log, and comprehensively reverse-engineer active payloads and C2 beaconing attempts.

QRadar-Inspired React SIEM.
